# Blog cover credits

The sources behind each blog cover, with the licence reported by Wikimedia Commons at the time of the
search and the source page for each file. The same attributions appear on the article pages themselves
(the `cover_credits` line in each post's frontmatter), because several of the photographs are CC BY and
that licence requires attribution where the work is shown.

Covers whose slot had no candidate that was not share-alike use an element made for this site instead;
those are marked below.

Every cover uses a searched photograph for its subject, its background and its ornament,
except where a slot had no candidate that was not share-alike: those three are generated
for this site. Licences are as reported by Wikimedia Commons at the time of the search.
